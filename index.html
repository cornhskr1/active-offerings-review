Nebraska Collegiate — Basketball Futures Cutoffs

Regular-season futures must be disabled before the first legitimate regular-season contest. A narrower subset used to determine the market outcome — such as away games, home games, conference games, or similar subsets — does not move that cutoff. Postseason futures must be disabled before the first legitimate postseason contest, if applicable. Combination markets must be removed by the earliest applicable cutoff among all included Nebraska collegiate components. Cutoff intelligence is schedule-driven and does not represent operator verification.
